Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking AARNA.

AARNA can comfortably accommodate 150 to 200 guests, making it ideal for intimate weddings, family gatherings, and mid-scale celebrations.

At present, dedicated on-site parking is not available. However, street parking is accessible in the surrounding area. We recommend that guests arrange their own transportation or use cab services for added convenience.

Yes, we have 1 guest room available for the bridal party or immediate family members to use on the day of the event.

You can reach us via phone, WhatsApp, or by filling out the enquiry form on this page. Our team will get in touch to confirm availability and discuss your requirements.

Yes, outside catering is welcome at AARNA. Our venue includes a fully equipped catering kitchen for your caterers to prepare and serve meals.

Yes, you are welcome to bring your own decorator. Our team can also assist with in-house decoration support to help bring your vision to life.

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance, especially for wedding dates during the peak season (October to February). Early booking ensures your preferred date is secured.
