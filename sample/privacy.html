Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how AARNA collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our event enquiry form or contact us via phone/WhatsApp, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, preferred event date, and specific celebration requirements to assist you with booking AARNA.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information provided solely to respond to your enquiries, check venue availability, coordinate event bookings, and provide relevant information regarding our hall facilities and amenities.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

AARNA does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. Enquiry form submissions are delivered via FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, and are accessed strictly by authorized hall management personnel for booking coordination.

4. Contacting Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, feel free to reach out to us at our address: 438, Thiruvottiyur High Rd, Korukkupet, Old Washermanpet, Chennai - 600021 or call us at +91 95001 11647.
